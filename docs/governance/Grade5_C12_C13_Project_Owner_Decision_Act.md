# Grade 5 C12/C13 — Project Owner Decision Act (Ratification)

**STATUS: ACCEPTED — DECISION ACT**

**Subject:** `Grade5_Arithmetic_Fluency_Draft_v0.1_Consolidated.md`, as
committed at `e02bb2a`, together with `stage3b_findings.md` and
`stage4_difficulty_spec.md` (same commit) — collectively "the C12/C13
spec chain."

## 1. What this Decision Act does

Under ADR-023 §4 (Decision Act requirements), the Project Owner reviews
the C12/C13 spec chain as committed at `e02bb2a` and records an explicit
decision that its content — taxonomy determinations for C12 and C13,
the generation-policy decisions in §§3–4 of the Consolidated document,
and the Stage 3B/Stage 4 empirical findings — is accepted as accurate
and authoritative, exactly as written.

## 2. What this Decision Act does not do

- It does not alter, narrow, extend, or reinterpret any content in the
  C12/C13 spec chain.
- It does not resolve C1's OPEN taxonomy-scope decision, C4's DEFERRED
  status, or the OPEN numeric-range status on C3/C5–C8.
- It does not introduce difficulty bands or reopen Stage 4's
  explicitly-excluded scoring/band work.
- It does not itself constitute an ADR-023 §6 Freeze Act.
- It does not authorize any Senior Phase candidate, and does not modify
  the Senior Phase Generation Policy, its methodology, or its approval
  record.

## 3. Basis for this decision

The existing C12/C13 specification chain contains the evidence,
methodology, generation-policy decisions, and empirical validation
recorded before ADR-023 established the formal Decision/Freeze
mechanism.

This Decision Act supplies the Project Owner decision required by
ADR-023 §4 for that existing content. It does not claim that the
original work was invalid; it records the Project Owner's present
decision under the governing mechanism now in force.

The Decision Act does not reopen or re-derive the underlying evidence
or methodology.

## 4. Decision

**Project Owner decision: ACCEPTED.**

The Project Owner hereby ratifies the C12/C13 specification content
identified above, exactly as it stands at `e02bb2a`, for purposes of
the subsequent ADR-023 §6 Freeze Act process.

This ratification does not itself perform the Freeze Act and does not
grant implementation authorization.

## 5. Scope boundary

This Decision Act applies only to Grade 5 C12/C13 specification
content contained in the identified specification chain.

It does not:

- authorize C1;
- authorize C4;
- resolve C3/C5/C6/C7/C8 numeric-range questions;
- authorize any difficulty band, cutoff, or score;
- authorize any new Grade 5 candidate;
- authorize any Senior Phase Mental Maths candidate;
- authorize changes to implementation, dispatch, generators, resolver
  logic, tests, or `AUTHORIZED_FAMILIES`.

## 6. Governance status

This Decision Act is a distinct ADR-023 §4 governance act.

A separate ADR-023 §6 Freeze Act is required before the C12/C13
specification content can be described as formally frozen under the
current governance mechanism.

**Decision Act status: ACCEPTED.**
