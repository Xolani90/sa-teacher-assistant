## 1. Exact content being frozen

**C12 (§3 of the Consolidated document — Status: CLOSED at the
generation-policy level):**
- Operand digit tier: 2-, 3-, or 4-digit, equal probability.
- Pair structure: matched-length (both operands same tier).
- Subtraction ordering: constructive (`a = max(x,y)`, `b = min(x,y)`).
- Equal-operand subtraction draw: discarded (mechanical consequence of
  the result floor, not a new exclusion).
- Result range: 10–9,999, enforced as an independent constraint.
- 5-digit result: forbidden.
- No separate operation-rebalancing mechanism (50/50 balance is a
  natural consequence of constructive ordering).

**C13 (§4 of the Consolidated document — Status: magnitude envelope
LOCKED (provisional); sampling policy CLOSED):**
- Factor `a`: 10–99. Factor `b`: 2–9. Uniform, independent draw.
- Structural form: Block B paired construction, `a × b = □ therefore
  □ = c ÷ d`, unknown in result position only, both sentences.
- Mandatory arithmetic consistency (`c = a×b`, `d = b`, quotient `= a`)
  and exact division (`c % d == 0`), guaranteed by construction.
- Seven numeric guards (`a≠0, b≠0, a≠1, b≠1, divisor≠1,
  divisor≠dividend, a≠b`) — retained in the formal rule set, confirmed
  structurally unreachable at the current envelope.
- **The magnitude envelope itself (10–99 × 2–9) remains LOCKED
  (provisional), not CLOSED, per the specification's own §4 status
  label.** This freeze act preserves that provisional status; it does
  not convert it to CLOSED, and does not itself constitute the
  "downstream difficulty/generation-spec validation" the specification
  names as the prerequisite for finalizing it.

**Explicitly not frozen by this act** (per the taxonomy table, §2 of the
Consolidated document): C12 and C13's own **taxonomy-level** numeric-range
status is listed there as **OPEN** ("see Section 3/4 for generation-stage
magnitude, which is separate"). This freeze act freezes the generation-stage
magnitude decisions in §§3–4 above; it does not resolve, close, or
otherwise touch that separate, taxonomy-level OPEN item.

## 3. ADR-023 §6(3) — Evidence vs. governance judgment, explicitly distinguished

**Evidence-derived** (from the Stage 3B corpora, independently
reproducible from `c12_corpus.csv`/`c13_corpus.csv` and `generate.py`):
- C12: ~50/50 addition/subtraction balance achieved via constructive
  ordering (no separate rebalancing mechanism); 85.9% acceptance
  efficiency; observed operand-tier distribution ~34%/39%/27%; observed
  result-tier distribution ~27%/38%/35%; the `<20`-operand
  characteristic (~7%, FLAGGED).
- C13: 100% acceptance rate (0% rejection) confirmed via audit; all
  seven numeric guards confirmed structurally unreachable across a
  100,000-attempt instrumented trace; observed product-digit
  distribution 14.8%/85.2%/0% (2-/3-/4-digit, FLAGGED); flat `a`/`b`
  draw distribution across the full declared range.

**Governance judgment** (Project Owner / prior-session decisions, not
themselves CAPS- or empirically-derived, though sometimes informed by
such evidence):
- The taxonomy separation of C12 and C13 as distinct named candidates
  (a Block-B-paired-form structural choice, not a CAPS-mandated split).
- The choice of matched-length operand tiers for C12 over the tested
  balanced-independent alternative (informed by, but not dictated by,
  the mechanism audit).
- The constructive-subtraction-ordering rule itself, as a design choice
  to restore operation balance.
- The `a≠b` guard for C13, explicitly recorded in the source
  specification as "a generation-design constraint preserving Block B's
  factor-pair fluency character... **not** a taxonomy/Block-A exclusion."
- The decision to lock C13's envelope at 10–99×2–9 rather than pursuing
  CAPS' demonstrated 4-digit coverage — explicitly a trade-off judgment
  ("preserving computational character... outweighed reaching CAPS'
  demonstrated 4-digit coverage"), not an evidence-compelled outcome.
- The decision to leave that envelope choice as LOCKED (provisional)
  rather than CLOSED, pending a downstream validation stage not yet
  opened.
