## 6. What this does NOT close

- The scope question itself, recorded as **C — explicitly OPEN** in
  `ratioSharing_Scope_Decision_Review.md`, remains OPEN. Having both
  evidence sets available does not by itself indicate whether
  `ratioSharing` and `ratioRate` should remain separate candidates, be
  merged, or be split differently along the CAPS sibling-bullet
  boundaries. That is a Layer 2 judgment this document does not make.
- G9 continuity for the sharing-in-a-ratio mechanism specifically
  remains unresolved — `ratioRate`'s checkpoint does not address it,
  because `ratioRate_Evidence_Review_Checkpoint.md` §2 explicitly
  excludes ratioSharing content from its own evidence set, and
  `ratioSharing_Evidence_Review_Checkpoint.md` itself recorded G9 as
  genuinely unresolved rather than merely unretrieved.
- Whether a worked example for sharing-in-a-ratio exists elsewhere in
  CAPS (assessment exemplars, etc.) remains unsearched, as recorded in
  `ratioSharing_Evidence_Review_Checkpoint.md` §"What remains
  outstanding."
- The basis for the existing `ratioSharing`/`ratioRate` split in
  `AUTHORIZED_FAMILIES` naming remains unestablished by any CAPS or
  governance evidence located to date. This document does not supply
  one.
