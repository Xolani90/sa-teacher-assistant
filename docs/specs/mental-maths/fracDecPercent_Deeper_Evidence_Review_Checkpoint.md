## Finding that revises the prior evidence boundary: Decimal Fractions is a standalone CAPS topic

`fracDecPercent_Evidence_Review_Checkpoint.md` stated that "CAPS does
not evidence 'decimal fractions' as a freestanding fourth sub-topic"
and that decimals appear "only as one representation inside Equivalent
Forms and inside Calculations with Fractions' broader scope." This
pass locates that statement was made on an incomplete sample. CAPS
Section 2 (Numbers, Operations and Relationships) contains a
**separate, numbered topic "1.5 Decimal fractions"**, distinct from
"1.4" (which covers Ordering/Comparing Fractions, Calculations with
Fractions, Percentages, and Equivalent Forms). "1.5 Decimal fractions"
has:

- its own phase-overview row at G7, G8, and G9 (Section 2 table,
  independently located and read in this pass),
- its own clarification-notes pages at each grade, with an explicit
  time allocation stated in the source (G7: 9 hours; G9 total for
  decimal fractions: 4,5 hours — G8's allocation was not independently
  read as a discrete figure in this pass),
- its own "Ordering and comparing," "Calculations with," "Calculation
  techniques," "Solving problems," and "Equivalent forms" sub-bullets
  at each grade, structurally paralleling how "1.4 Common fractions"
  is organized.

This is a correction to the evidence record, not a scope decision:
decimals are not being declared their own taxonomy candidate here —
only that the earlier "no standalone sub-topic" finding is now known
to be incomplete, and any future scope decision must account for
"Decimal Fractions" as a fourth CAPS-named unit alongside Calculations
with Fractions, Percentages, and Equivalent Forms, not fold it
silently into the latter.

## Cross-cutting finding: G8 is the richest evidence grade across all three/four sub-topics

Across Calculations with Fractions, Percentages, and Decimal
Fractions, **G8 clarification notes consistently carry the most and
most varied worked examples** in this pass — full addition, division,
and (for decimals) squares/roots demonstrations that G7 and G9 either
lack or only partially cover. This is stated as an observation about
where this pass located evidence, not as a claim that G7/G9 worked
examples do not exist elsewhere in the document (e.g. assessment
exemplars, sections not sampled in this pass).
