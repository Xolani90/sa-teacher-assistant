## 3. Grade 7 evidence

### 3.1 CAPS specification

In the Grade 7 specification for Whole Numbers, under **Mental calculations**,
CAPS states:

> Multiplication of whole numbers to at least 12 × 12

The same Grade 7 material places this within the Mental calculations
framework and describes mental calculations as an opportunity to consolidate
learners' number knowledge, including number facts and calculation
techniques.

**Evidence classification:** explicit mental-calculation curriculum evidence.

**Grade:** G7.

**Content:** multiplication of whole numbers to at least 12 × 12.

**Source location:** CAPS Senior Phase Mathematics Grades 7–9, Grade 7
Whole Numbers / Mental calculations; Section 2 specification and Grade 7
clarification material.

---

## 4. Grade 8 evidence

### 4.1 CAPS specification

In the Grade 8 specification for Whole Numbers, under **Mental calculations**,
CAPS states:

> Revise multiplication of whole numbers to at least 12 × 12

The Grade 8 clarification of content likewise identifies multiplication of
whole numbers to at least 12 × 12 as material to be revised.

This is an explicit Grade 8 occurrence and is not inferred solely from the
Grade 7 occurrence.

**Evidence classification:** explicit mental-calculation curriculum evidence,
with explicit revision framing.

**Grade:** G8.

**Content:** multiplication of whole numbers to at least 12 × 12.

**Source location:** CAPS Senior Phase Mathematics Grades 7–9, Grade 8
Whole Numbers / Mental calculations, including §3.3.2.

---

## 5. Grade 9 evidence

### 5.1 Absence of the specific 12 × 12 multiplication-fact continuation

The Grade 9 Whole Numbers material does not contain a corresponding
Grade 9 continuation stating that learners should revise or practise
multiplication of whole numbers to at least 12 × 12.

The Grade 9 Whole Numbers topic is presented under **Properties of numbers**
rather than the Grade 7/8 top-level **Mental calculations** heading.

Grade 9 does contain separate calculation-technique language concerning
written and mental calculations of whole numbers. That general calculation
language is not, by itself, evidence that the specific
`multiplicationFactFluency` construct — multiplication facts to at least
12 × 12 — continues into Grade 9.

Accordingly, this checkpoint records only the absence of a located
Grade 9-specific 12 × 12 multiplication-fact requirement.

**Evidence classification:** no located Grade 9-specific continuation of the
12 × 12 multiplication-fact requirement.

**Grade:** G9.

**Specific construct:** multiplication of whole numbers to at least 12 × 12.

## 7. Evidence-only conclusion

The retrospective evidence record establishes the following factual evidence:

1. CAPS explicitly specifies multiplication of whole numbers to at least
   12 × 12 in Grade 7 under Mental calculations.
2. CAPS explicitly specifies revision of multiplication of whole numbers to
   at least 12 × 12 in Grade 8 under Mental calculations.
3. No corresponding Grade 9-specific statement requiring multiplication of
   whole numbers to at least 12 × 12 was located in the reviewed CAPS
   material.
4. General Grade 9 written-and-mental-calculation language is not treated
   here as evidence of continuation of the specific 12 × 12
   multiplication-fact construct.

These statements document evidence only.
