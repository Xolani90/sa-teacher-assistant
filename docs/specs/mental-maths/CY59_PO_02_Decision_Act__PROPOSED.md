---

## 1. Decision

**Question:**

What is the governance relationship between `ratioSharing` and `ratioRate`?

**Project Owner decision:**

> **A — Distinct sibling candidates.**

`ratioSharing` and `ratioRate` are governed as separate candidates despite
their combined presentation in the G9 curriculum.

---

## 2. Rationale

The Cycle 59 evidence audit established materially distinct G7/G8 curriculum
content:

* `ratioSharing`: sharing a given whole in a given ratio.
* `ratioRate`: comparing two quantities of different kinds, including rate
  contexts such as speed, distance and time.

At G9, the curriculum presents ratio and rate together in a combined bullet
with worked examples. However, that curriculum presentation does not itself
require the two mathematical constructs to become a single governance
candidate.

The Project Owner therefore accepts the distinct-sibling structure.

This preserves the explicit G7/G8 construct distinction while allowing the
curriculum's G9 combined presentation to be represented as shared curriculum
context rather than forced candidate identity.

---

## 3. Consequences

This decision means:

* `ratioSharing` remains a distinct governance candidate.
* `ratioRate` remains a distinct governance candidate.
* Their generation-eligibility specifications may be developed independently.
* Their grade-scope authorizations remain independently governed.
* The G9 combined curriculum presentation does not automatically merge the
  two candidates.
* Any future G9 authorization for either candidate must still be established
  through the appropriate governance process.

---

## 4. Existing Scope State Remains Unchanged

This decision does NOT itself grant new grade authorization.

In particular:

* existing `ratioSharing` G7 authorization remains unchanged;
* unresolved `ratioSharing` G9 status remains unresolved;
* `ratioRate` remains without an accepted scope authorization unless and until
  separately authorized through the appropriate governance process.

Candidate identity and grade authorization are separate governance questions.

---

## 5. Explicit Non-Actions

This Decision Act does NOT:

* authorize `ratioRate` at any grade;
* authorize `ratioSharing` at any additional grade;
* resolve the existing `ratioSharing` G9 scope question;
* establish numeric ranges;
* establish ratio-pair ranges;
* establish total ranges;
* establish multiplier ranges;
* establish simplification rules;
* establish 1:1 treatment;
* establish item forms;
* establish exactness rules;
* establish `canonicalAnswer` semantics;
* establish prompt semantics;
* establish randomization or weighting;
* establish difficulty bands;
* authorize production implementation;
* modify `AUTHORIZED_FAMILIES`;
* freeze the Senior Phase Generation Policy;
* make the Generation Policy Accepted.

Any current implementation behavior remains implementation-observed only
unless separately authorized.
