# CY56-PO-01 — Project Owner Decision Act

**Status:** ACCEPTED
**Accepted by:** Xolani Tshabalala, Project Owner
**Acceptance date:** 2026-09-05
**Depends on:** ADR-023 (Accepted, bootstrap commit `580fa45`)
**Related decision:** CY54-PO-01 = B — Umbrella / sub-construct

This document records the explicit Project Owner Decision Act required by
ADR-023 §4.

I, **Xolani Tshabalala, Project Owner**, hereby ACCEPT the following decision
under ADR-023:

* **CY56-PO-01 = B — No separate umbrella-level scope record is required at
  this time.**

The `mulDivFluency` umbrella's currently evidenced governance scope is
satisfied by the existing governed sub-construct record for
`multiplicationFactFluency`, together with the already accepted
umbrella/sub-construct relationship established by CY54-PO-01.

This decision does not authorize any new mathematical content.

---

## 1. Decision

**Question:**

Does the `mulDivFluency` umbrella require its own independent grade-scope
decision record, distinct from the existing
`multiplicationFactFluency` G7–G8 scope decision record?

**Project Owner decision:**

> **B — No separate umbrella-level scope record is required at this time.**

The existing governance structure is sufficient while the umbrella contains
no independently authorized content beyond its governed sub-construct.

---

## 2. Rationale

The Cycle 57 evidence audit found no CAPS bullet, accepted scope decision
record, or evidence checkpoint that currently assigns independent mathematical
content to `mulDivFluency` beyond:

1. the accepted `multiplicationFactFluency` sub-construct; and
2. the multiplication/division inverse-operation content whose ownership
   remains unresolved under CY54-PO-02.

Creating an umbrella-level scope record at this stage would authorize no
additional content and would duplicate governance without establishing a
substantive scope boundary.

Therefore the Project Owner accepts **Option B**.

---

## 3. Consequences

This decision means:

* `mulDivFluency` remains the accepted umbrella/family identifier.
* `multiplicationFactFluency` remains its separately governed sub-construct.
* The existing G7–G8 authorization of `multiplicationFactFluency` remains
  unchanged.
* No independent umbrella-level generation eligibility is authorized.
* No new mathematical content is authorized.
* No numeric or operand ranges are authorized.
* No item forms are authorized.
* No exactness rules are authorized.
* No `canonicalAnswer` semantics are authorized.
* No prompt semantics are authorized.
* No randomization constraints are authorized.
* No production implementation is authorized.

If future governance assigns independently authorized content to the
`mulDivFluency` umbrella, a separate scope record may be created at that time
if required by ADR-022/ADR-023.

---

## 4. CY54-PO-02 Remains Unchanged

This decision does **not** resolve CY54-PO-02.

The multiplication/division inverse-operation ownership boundary remains:

> **D — Remain unresolved pending further evidence.**

No assumption is authorized that the inverse-operation content belongs to:

* `mulDivFluency`;
* `addSub`; or
* a separate candidate.

No generation-eligibility specification may assign that content to any
candidate until the ownership question is separately resolved.

---

## 5. Explicit Non-Actions

This Decision Act does not:

* modify `AUTHORIZED_FAMILIES`;

* establish exactness rules;

* establish prompt semantics;
* establish randomization constraints;
* freeze the Senior Phase Generation Policy;
* mark the Generation Policy Accepted; or
* make any implementation implementation-authoritative.
