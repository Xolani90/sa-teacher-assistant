SA Teacher Assistant

CAPS-Aligned Classroom Materials, Delivered on WhatsApp

SA Teacher Assistant is a WhatsApp-based AI assistant built for South African teachers. Send a message describing what you need — a lesson plan, worksheet, test, or explanation — and receive CAPS-aligned classroom materials in seconds, without leaving WhatsApp.

What we offer

  • Lesson plans aligned to the CAPS curriculum, generated on request
  • Worksheets and tests (with memoranda) for South African classrooms
  • Learner-friendly explanations of curriculum topics
  • Classroom observation, assessment, and reporting tools for teachers
  • Everything delivered directly via WhatsApp — no app to install

Pricing

A free tier is available for teachers to try the service. Continued use beyond the free tier is available on the Pro plan at R99/month, billed via secure card payment.

Who it's for

South African teachers across primary and secondary phases who want to save time on lesson preparation, assessment design, and classroom administration.

Contact

For business or support enquiries, reach us via WhatsApp through the number provided at sign-up, or see our Privacy Policy for how we handle your data.
