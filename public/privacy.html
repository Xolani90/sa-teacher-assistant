SA Teacher Assistant

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 June 2025  ·  Last updated: 10 June 2025

SA Teacher Assistant ("we", "us", "our") is a WhatsApp-based AI service that helps South African teachers generate CAPS-aligned educational content. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information in compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with SA Teacher Assistant via WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number (stored as a one-way cryptographic hash — we cannot reverse it to your original number)
  • Messages you send to the service (used solely to generate your requested content)
  • Usage data: number of requests made, subscription tier, and timestamps
  • Payment confirmation data received from our payment processor (we do not store card details)

We do not collect your name, email address, identity number, or any other personal information unless you voluntarily provide it in a message.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Respond to your content generation requests via WhatsApp
  • Track your usage quota and enforce free/paid tier limits
  • Process and verify payments
  • Improve the quality and accuracy of the service
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate SA Teacher Assistant:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business API) — message delivery. Subject to Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic — AI content generation. Messages are processed per Anthropic's usage policies.
  • PayFast — payment processing. PayFast is PCI-DSS compliant and does not share card data with us.
  • Render — cloud hosting for our application infrastructure.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your phone number is stored only as a hashed value using a one-way cryptographic function. We cannot retrieve your original number from this hash. Usage records are stored in a secured database hosted on Render's infrastructure in the United States.

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

5. Data Retention

We retain usage records for a maximum of 12 months from your last interaction with the service. Message content is not stored beyond the time required to generate your response. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 7).

6. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Object to the processing of your personal information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

7. Contact Us

To exercise your rights or ask questions about this policy, contact us via WhatsApp or email:

  • WhatsApp: message us directly on the SA Teacher Assistant number
  • Email: privacy@sateacherassistant.co.za

Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent version. Continued use of the service after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
